Privacy Policy

Selector - Visual Element Picker
Last updated: November 21, 2026

Page Data and Prompts

Selector processes page data in your browser to help you select elements and copy a structured prompt. The prompt can include the page URL, element selectors, visible text, HTML snippets, framework metadata, and annotations you enter. When you copy the prompt, Selector writes it to your local clipboard.

Optional Screenshots

Text-only copying is the default. On supported browsers, check Include screenshot of active selection, click Copy Prompt, and approve sharing the current tab in the browser's sharing dialog. Selector crops a frame to the visible area of the active element and adds a PNG to the clipboard alongside the prompt. The crop can include content rendered over the selected area. Select a different element or leave the screenshot option unchecked if you want to exclude that content.

Permissions and Local Storage

Selector requests access to webpages so its picker can inspect elements on sites where you use it. The storage permission saves the launcher position on your device. Selector does not store prompts or screenshots in extension storage.

Sharing

Selector processes prompts and screenshots on your device. It does not upload them or send page content to a server. Selector has no backend, analytics, or tracking. If you paste clipboard content into another website or app, that service receives the content you choose to paste.
